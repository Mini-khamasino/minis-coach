Mini Apps — Privacy

Applies to Mini's Coach and the other Mini Apps. Last updated: October 25, 2026.

What we store

  • What you enter: food, water, weight, workouts, your profile (name, age, height, goal) and settings.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email and profile photo, used only to show who is signed in and to keep your data in your own account.

Problem reports and feedback

If the app hits an error, it sends a short technical report (error message, app version, device type) so it can be fixed — never your food, weight or notes. Feedback you choose to send is stored the same way. Only the developer can read them.

Where it lives

Your data is stored on your phone. If you sign in, a copy is kept in Google Firebase under your account so you can restore it on another phone. Only you can read it; security rules block everyone else.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no selling or sharing your data.
  • The AI assistant (when on) sends your message and anonymous numbers (age, weight, food log — never your name, email or photo) to Groq, which is set not to store them. Personal topics such as health, medicines or relationships never leave your phone. The "Ask an AI" button only copies text that you choose to paste somewhere.

Deleting your data

Sign out and clear the app's site data on your phone. To delete the cloud copy, contact the developer below and it will be removed.
